\title[Blueprint of the Lean formalization]{Comparison principles for degenerate sub-elliptic equations in non-divergence form: blueprint of the Lean formalization}
\author{Juan J. Manfredi}
\author{Shirsho Mukherjee}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
This document collects the natural-language statements and detailed proofs that accompany the Lean~4 formalization of Theorems~1.2 and~1.3 of J.~J.~Manfredi and S.~Mukherjee, \emph{Comparison principles for degenerate sub-elliptic equations in non-divergence form}.
Five results that the paper cites from the literature (the strong maximum and minimum principles of Bardi and Goffi in the form of Corollary~2.4, Aleksandrov's theorem, Jensen's lemma, Pansu's differentiability theorem with [DMV13, Proposition~4.8], and parts (1)--(3) of Lemma~3.8, which are [CYW07, Proposition~3.3]) are not proved; they are stated precisely and carried as explicit hypotheses (\texttt{CitedResults}, \texttt{EuclidCitedResults}). Everything else is proved.
The Carnot group enters through the structure \texttt{CarnotSetting}, whose fields are the structural facts of Section~2.4 of the paper.
Each subsection corresponds to the Lean declaration of the same name in the directory \texttt{CompND/}; cross-references point to the results that a proof uses.
Section~\ref{sec:main} lists the formalized results of the paper.
Within each section, items appear in dependency order.
\end{abstract}
