\begin{definition}[Symmetrized horizontal second derivative of a $2$-jet, (2.6)]
Let $n,m\in\mathbb N$, let
$\sigma:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^{n\times m}$, $\sigma(x)=[\sigma^1(x),\ldots,\sigma^m(x)]$,
be a frame of $m$ vector fields on $\mathbb R^n$, let $x\in\mathbb R^n$ and let $(D,B)$ be
a $1$-jet and a $2$-jet on $\mathbb R^n$. The \emph{symmetrized horizontal second
derivative of $(D,B)$ at $x$} is the matrix
$\mathrm{hhessS}\ \sigma\ x\ D\ B\in\mathbb R^{m\times m}$ with entries
\[
 \big(\mathrm{hhessS}\ \sigma\ x\ D\ B\big)_{i,j}
 \ =\ B\big(\sigma^{i}(x),\sigma^{j}(x)\big)\ +\ \mathcal M(x,D)_{i,j},
\]
where $\mathcal M(x,D)$ is the correction matrix of \noderef{Mfield}.

If $u$ is twice differentiable at $x$ in the pointwise (Peano) sense, with second-order
jet $(D,B)=(\nabla u(x),D^2u(x))$, then $B(\sigma^i(x),\sigma^j(x))=\langle
D^2u(x)\sigma^i(x),\sigma^j(x)\rangle=(\sigma(x)^TD^2u(x)\,\sigma(x))_{i,j}$, so
\[
 \mathrm{hhessS}\ \sigma\ x\ D\ B
 \ =\ \sigma(x)^TD^2u(x)\,\sigma(x)+\mathcal M(x,\nabla u(x))
 \ =\ \mathcal X\mathcal X^{s}u(x),
\]
the symmetrized horizontal second derivative
$\tfrac12\big(X_iX_ju+X_jX_iu\big)_{i,j}$ of (2.6). The matrix is symmetric whenever
$B$ is, since $\mathcal M(x,D)$ is symmetric by construction.
\end{definition}
