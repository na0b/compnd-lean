\begin{definition}
Let $\mathbb G$ be a sub-elliptic setting (\noderef{CarnotSetting}) with group law $*$, let
$\Om\subseteq\mathbb R^{n}$, let $\delta\in\mathbb R$, let
$u,v:\mathbb R^{n}\to\mathbb R$ and let $h\in\mathbb R^{n}$. Writing $u_h:=u(\cdot*h)$ for
the right translate of $u$ by $h$ and $\Om_\delta$ for the inner domain
(\noderef{TranslatedDomain}), the first quantity of (3.9) is
\[
 M_\delta(h):=\sup_{x\in\Om_\delta}\big\{u_h(x)-v(x)\big\}
 =\sup_{x\in\Om_\delta}\big\{u(x*h)-v(x)\big\} .
\]
The supremum is the least upper bound in $\mathbb R$; it is a genuine supremum of a
non-empty set of reals bounded above exactly when $\Om_\delta\neq\emptyset$ and $u_h-v$ is
bounded above on $\Om_\delta$, which is the situation in every use below.
\end{definition}
