\begin{lemma}[Positivity of $\mathcal E$ under (E), and $2$-homogeneity of the quadratic form]
Let $m\in\mathbb N$, let $A:\mathbb R^{m}\to\mathbb R^{m\times m}$ and
$H:\mathbb R^{m}\to\mathbb R$, and assume $(A,H)$ satisfies the structure conditions (E) and
(S) of section Section~1.2 (\noderef{StructureConditionsES}). Then, with $\mathcal E$ the
ellipticity function (1.9) of \noderef{Ellip}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathcal E_A(\eta)>0$ for every $\eta\in\mathbb R^{m}$ with $\eta\ne0$;
\item for every matrix $M\in\mathbb R^{m\times m}$, every $s\in\mathbb R$ and every
  $\eta\in\mathbb R^{m}$,
  \[
   \sum_{i}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}(s\eta)_j(s\eta)_i\ =\ s^{2}\sum_{i}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i .
  \]
\end{enumerate}
(Part (2) uses no hypothesis on $A$, $H$ or $M$: it is the $2$-homogeneity of a quadratic
form in its vector slot, the matrix being held fixed.)
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Part (1).} By \noderef{Ellip}, the ellipticity function (1.9) is by
definition the double sum
\[
 \mathcal E_A(\eta)=\langle A(\eta)\eta,\eta\rangle=\sum_{i}\sum_{j}A(\eta)_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i .
\]
Condition (E) of \noderef{StructureConditionsES} asserts precisely that
$\sum_{i}\sum_{j}A(\eta)_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i>0$ for every $\eta\ne0$. The two expressions are
the same, so (1) is condition (E) read through the definition of $\mathcal E$.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part (2).} Fix $M$, $s$ and $\eta$. Scalar multiplication on
$\mathbb R^{m}$ is coordinatewise, so $(s\eta)_k=s\,\eta_k$ for every index $k$. Hence, for
each pair $(i,j)$,
\[
 M_{i,j}(s\eta)_j(s\eta)_i=M_{i,j}\,(s\eta_j)(s\eta_i)=s^{2}\,M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i ,
\]
by commutativity and associativity of multiplication in $\mathbb R$. Both index sets are
finite, so multiplication by the constant $s^{2}$ distributes over the sums: for each fixed
$i$,
\[
 \sum_{j}M_{i,j}(s\eta)_j(s\eta)_i=\sum_{j}s^{2}M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i
  =s^{2}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i ,
\]
and summing these identities over $i$, again pulling the constant $s^{2}$ out of the finite
outer sum,
\[
 \sum_{i}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}(s\eta)_j(s\eta)_i
  =\sum_{i}s^{2}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i
  =s^{2}\sum_{i}\sum_{j}M_{i,j}\eta_j\eta_i ,
\]
which is the asserted identity.
\end{proof}
