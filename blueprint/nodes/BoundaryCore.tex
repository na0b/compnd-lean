\begin{definition}
Let $\mathbb G$ be a sub-elliptic setting (\noderef{CarnotSetting}) with group law $*$ and
homogeneous norm $\|\cdot\|$, and write $d(x,y):=\|y^{-1}*x\|$ for the left-invariant
distance of Section~2.4. For $\Om\subseteq\mathbb R^{n}$ and $\rho\in\mathbb R$ the
\emph{compact core} $K_\rho$ of (3.8) is
\[
 K_\rho:=\big\{x\in\Om:\ \rho\le d(x,y)\ \text{for every}\ y\in\del\Om\big\} .
\]
The paper writes this as $\{x\in\Om:\ \dist(x,\del\Om)\ge\rho\}$ with
$\dist(x,\del\Om)=\inf\{d(x,y):y\in\del\Om\}$; since an infimum is $\ge\rho$ exactly
when every member of the set is $\ge\rho$, the two descriptions agree verbatim when
$\del\Om\ne\emptyset$, and for $\del\Om=\emptyset$ the displayed condition is vacuous,
giving $K_\rho=\Om$, which matches the paper's convention $\inf\emptyset=+\infty$.
\end{definition}
